Musicality - In a world where music holds power and substance, Masha, a fifteen year old girl aspires to be great composer just as her widowed mother. There is a duality, where music in the kingdom of Jam-topia is used for prosperity and the wellbeing of the people of the kingdom, but in the other sense, music is used for chaos and mayhem, far from what it was intended for by unruly fiends. After the great musical war, the prospers won and thus those foes were banished and went wandering off, thus seizing the malice in the kingdom . But an event occurred which led them to return and encroach ever so subtly, being much more stronger and menacing. Masha has to accept this reality and learn to tackle the challenges that come forth with this truth by growing, learning and unraveling this secrets behind this mystical art form and use her insight to fend off evil and restore back harmony in Jam-topia. Would she be able to bear such a monstrous task? Find out by reading Musicality.
